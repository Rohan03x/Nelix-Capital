Privacy Notice

How this application handles session, watchlist, model, and operational data.

Data We Process

Nelix Capital may process ticker searches, selected companies, valuation assumptions, watchlist entries, manual compare relationships, anonymous session identifiers, request metadata, cache keys, and diagnostic logs needed to operate and improve the service.

How Data Is Used

Data is used to render valuation pages, remember anonymous workflow state, reduce repeated provider calls, improve ticker discovery, maintain learning evidence, investigate failures, and protect the service from abuse.

Financial Data Caches

Company fundamentals, prices, exchange symbols, and model fixtures are generally about public companies, not private individuals. They may be cached so the app can remain responsive and resilient when providers are slow or unavailable.

Retention

Anonymous workflow data such as watchlists, notes, compare history, and diagnostic logs should be retained only as long as needed for product operation, debugging, security, and model-quality review. The production retention target for user-generated workflow data is no longer than 24 months unless a shorter legal, security, or operational period applies.

Your Choices

You can clear browser storage and cookies to remove local session state. Hosted deployments expose DELETE /api/me to purge anonymous server-side workflow data such as watchlists, search impressions, manual compare history, and derived compare relationships.

Security

The application uses session controls, CSRF checks, rate limiting, and input-size guards where enabled. No internet-facing system can be guaranteed secure, so avoid entering sensitive personal, account, or brokerage information into valuation notes or overrides.
