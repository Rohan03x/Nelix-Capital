Disclosures

Important information about valuation methodology, source data, limitations, and conflicts.

Valuation Methodology

Nelix Capital may use discounted cash flow models, peer multiples, scenario analysis, sensitivity tables, analyst consensus references, quality checks, and learning overlays. These are analytical estimates, not statements of fair value, market value, or future performance.

Data Sources and Freshness

The application may combine market data, company fundamentals, exchange metadata, analyst estimates, cached records, curated demo fixtures, and user-entered assumptions. Prices and financial statements can be delayed, restated, stale, adjusted, or unavailable across jurisdictions and exchanges.

Forecast Risk

Valuation outputs are highly sensitive to revenue growth, margins, reinvestment, terminal value, discount rates, capital structure, share count, currency conversion, and peer selection. Small assumption changes can produce materially different intrinsic-value estimates.

Automated Analysis

Explainability notes, confidence scores, and generated commentary may summarize model inputs and risks, but they can omit context or make mistakes. They should be treated as prompts for review rather than definitive conclusions.

No Performance Guarantee

Historical backtests, calibration metrics, peer relationships, and prior model behaviour do not guarantee future returns, future forecast accuracy, or future model performance.

Conflicts

Unless separately disclosed in writing, Nelix Capital does not receive issuer compensation for coverage and does not execute trades. Users may hold positions in securities analysed by the tool.
